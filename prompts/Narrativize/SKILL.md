---
name: narrativize
description: narrativize converts an abstract concept, claim, principle, mechanism, or scenario into a concrete, lived, time-extended narrative. it instantiates the idea through a character, setting, pressures, choices, and consequences. unlike simulate (analytical projection) or reframe (conceptual restatement), narrativize generates a phenomenological “what it would feel like” story that reveals hidden dynamics, frictions, and surprises.
---

## tl;dr

**narrativize ≠ illustrate.**

- **illustrate** → give an example or vignette  
- **narrativize** → build a whole *trajectory* with agency, pressures, and emergent detail  

narrativize asks:

> “if this idea were lived by an actual human (or agent) over time, what would unfold — and what would we learn from the unfolding?”

it’s a **concretization operator** with **temporal dynamics**.

---

## when to use

use **narrativize** when you want:

- to test if an abstract idea is **coherent in practice**  
- to reveal **frictions, delays, path-dependencies, or emotions** absent in the abstraction  
- to see how incentives and constraints **actually show up phenomenologically**  
- to create **gut-level clarity** on a concept’s implications  
- to surface **dynamics you would never see via pure reasoning**  
- to prototype “lived consequences” before committing to a strategy or worldview

don’t use when:

- you want rigorous forecasting → **simulate**  
- you want conceptual variation → **diverge**  
- you want assumption structure → **excavate**  
- you want causal pathways → **backchain**

rule of thumb:  
**narrativize = embody the idea in a life.**

## narrative types (menu)

### **agent-trajectory**
follow one character’s choices, constraints, expectations, surprises.

### **dyad or small-group microdrama**
show tensions, miscommunications, aligned/disaligned incentives.

### **institutional slice**
place the concept inside an organization and watch roles interact.

### **ecosystem drift**
show how multiple agents adapt over time under pressures.

### **moment-to-moment phenomenology**
zoom tightly on cognition, emotion, sensory detail to test what the abstraction *feels like*.

you may mix modes.

---

## signature

narrativize(seed, horizon?, perspective?, constraints?) → narrative + insights

- **seed:** abstract idea, claim, scenario, mechanism, or principle  
- **horizon:** timescale (minutes → decades)  
- **perspective:** 1st-person, 3rd-person, omniscient, rotating  
- **constraints:** optional structure (e.g., “only internal monologue”, “only dialogue”, “must include breaking point”, “no moralizing”)

output includes:

- the **narrative**  
- a **distillation**: what the narrative revealed that abstraction didn’t  
- **hidden frictions**, **emergent dynamics**, or **surprises**

---

## process (step by step)

### step 0: extract conceptual core  
understand what must be instantiated: the idea’s primitives, normative intent, incentives, or mechanism.

### step 1: choose narrative container  
pick character(s), setting, and timescale that naturally stress-test the abstraction.

### step 2: scaffold pressures & constraints  
identify forces acting on the character(s):

- incentives  
- norms  
- personal limitations  
- environmental constraints  
- emotional tone  
- uncertainty or conflict

### step 3: let the trajectory run  
allow the story to unfold:

- early expectations  
- mid-course corrections  
- friction points  
- surprising outcomes  
- second-order effects  
- unintended consequences

no deus ex machina; everything must arise from pressures + character traits.

### step 4: surface phenomenology  
bring in sensory, cognitive, or emotional detail that the abstract concept masks.  
but **do not** turn into melodrama; aim for *diagnostic realism*, not fiction fluff.

### step 5: extract insights  
after the narrative:

- what showed up that the abstraction ignored?  
- what became harder / easier?  
- what was surprising?  
- what hidden variables dominated?  
- what failure mode emerged?  
- what nuance would you feed back into the conceptual model?

### step 6: label narrative stance  
flag narrative style:

- `[LOW-CONFIDENCE]` if speculative  
- `[HYPOTHETICAL]` if counterfactual  
- `[PHENOMENOLOGICAL]` if sensory/cognitive focused  
- `[ARCHETYPAL]` if representative rather than specific  
- `[LIMIT CASE]` if intentionally extreme

---

## quality criteria

**coherence**
- [ ] the narrative respects incentives, constraints, and character traits  
- [ ] no magical thinking; causal continuity is preserved  

**diagnostic depth**
- [ ] surfaces frictions absent in abstraction  
- [ ] reveals hidden variables or dynamics  
- [ ] contains at least one surprise or reversal  

**phenomenological validity**
- [ ] includes lived detail (cognitive, emotional, sensory)  
- [ ] avoids melodramatic fluff or moralizing  

**post-hoc clarity**
- [ ] insights section identifies what abstraction missed  
- [ ] narrative is short enough to be digestible (2–6 paragraphs unless constrained otherwise)

## genre-specific patterns

### strategy / planning  
instantiate plan as a character executing it under deadlines, fatigue, politics.

### research / epistemics  
instantiate a scientist or analyst grappling with uncertainty, incentives, fatigue.

### institution design  
show how a policy interacts with human quirks, misaligned incentives, or unexpected norms.

### product design  
show the user’s lived journey, including confusion, frustration, delight.

### ethics / principles  
show how a value collides with reality in edge cases and compromises.

---

## anti-patterns

- **didactic fiction** — narrative becomes a sermon or moral demonstration  
- **wishful-storytelling** — events unfold too cleanly  
- **vagueness** — no concrete scenes or pressures  
- **mechanistic dryness** — reads like a spec, not a life  
- **omniscient safety** — no misunderstandings, no limited knowledge, no stakes

## integration with other ops

**upstream:**  
- diverge → generate several narrative starting frames  
- reframe → choose a lens to narrativize through  
- excavate → identify assumptions to instantiate  
- backchain → choose a causal path to dramatize  

**downstream:**  
- simulate → turn narrative pressures into quantitative forecasts  
- attack → find narrative failure modes  
- reframe → extract new frames from the story  
- future_memory → narrativize from future backward  
- prioritize → decide which insights matter

## examples (mini)

### example 1: narrativize “value drift through AI assistance”

**narrative (compressed)**  
Mira, a grad student, starts using an AI assistant for lit reviews.  
year 1: she saves hours; the assistant suggests framings that feel “cleaner.”  
year 3: she adopts the assistant's citation habits, argument shapes. colleagues praise her new clarity.  
year 5: she notices her drafts sound like “the model.” no big deal — until she rereads early notebooks and feels estranged from her former interests.  
year 7: she can’t tell whether her taste has matured or homogenized. a minor disagreement with a friend reveals she now defaults to the model’s style of reasoning. it’s subtle — but pervasive.

**insights**  
- drift happens via micro-conveniences, not big decisions  
- “better argumentation” feels good even when it shifts identity  
- internal taste is vulnerable to external scaffolding  
- no single moment felt like drift — the curve was smooth  

---

### example 2: narrativize “users rely too heavily on automation”

**narrative (compressed)**  
Leo manages ops queues. he adopts an auto-triage tool.  
week 2: he double-checks every decision.  
week 6: checking feels wasteful; he rubber-stamps.  
week 10: anomalies creep in — but the audit interface is clunky, so he ignores mild discomfort.  
week 14: a subtle mis-triage cascades, causing a near-failure. he blames himself, not the tool.  
week 20: leadership blames “operator vigilance.” Leo quietly realizes the system shaped his attention more than his choices shaped the system.

**insights**  
- delegation happens because of friction, not laziness  
- small interface decisions drive over-trust  
- mild discomfort signals get suppressed by throughput pressure  
- blame gets misassigned due to institutional structure
